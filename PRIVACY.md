# AI Governance Sources: Privacy Policy

## Scope

This policy describes the AI Governance Sources skills-only plugin published by Bossle CPA when used in Claude, ChatGPT or Codex. The plugin consists of instructions, curated obligation tables and bundled legal texts. It has no server, scripts, hooks, external API connections or telemetry.

## Personal data and purposes

The plugin asks for a description without personal data. Do not include names, contact details, identification numbers, credentials or other personal data. If personal data is supplied, the skill instructs the model to omit it from the answer. This does not remove that data from your conversation.

The model uses the description to find relevant provisions in the bundled files and produce an answer. The plugin has no mechanism for collecting your input or answers for Bossle CPA, and does not use them for advertising, profiling or analytics.

## Recipients

The plugin contains no mechanism for transmitting your input or answers to Bossle CPA or to another external service. It does not contact EUR-Lex when answering: the legal texts are bundled with the plugin.

Your conversation and any execution of the skill are handled by the AI platform you use: Anthropic for Claude, or OpenAI for ChatGPT and Codex. The platform provider's applicable privacy terms, product settings and workspace controls govern that processing. This policy does not replace them.

## Retention

The plugin maintains no separate database, logs or storage of your input or answers. There is therefore no separate plugin retention period.

The platform provider may retain conversations, files and other product data under its applicable policies and your account or workspace settings. Disabling or removing the plugin does not itself delete those records.

## Your controls

You can choose not to use the plugin, disable it or remove it. Avoid entering personal data. Use the privacy, data and deletion controls provided by your AI platform and, where applicable, your workspace administrator. Conversations, files and saved memories may have separate controls; removing one does not necessarily remove the others.

Bossle CPA cannot delete records held in your account with the platform provider through this plugin. For information about your platform provider's handling of data, consult the privacy information and data controls provided in the product you use.
